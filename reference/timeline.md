# Lady Pepper & Aizomea Timeline

This is the broad timeline reference. For facts revised since this outline, [the canon ledger](canon-ledger.md) takes precedence; for applied entry dates and trip windows, use [the chronology](chronology.md). Approximate dates remain approximate, and questions or possibilities below are not settled facts. The entry-order section is a historical proposal, not the current manuscript order.

## Key Dates

- 1864 Nangula Sossusvlei's birth
- 1901 Lady Indigo Pepper's birth
- 1923 Pepper meets Nangula (Pepper 22, Nangula 59)
- 1925 Pepper meets Cassius Worthington
- 1930 Cendre's birth
- 1933 River's birth
- 1935 Nangula Sossusvlei's death at 71

### The Trips

- **1938 --- Trip 1. Pepper alone. Age 37. ~1 year.**
  Cendre is 8, River is 5. Cassius holds the fort.
  Three years after Nangula's death --- enough time to solve the final puzzle, the compass, the humming dragon on the windowsill. The discovery trip. Everything is new. She arrives terrified, disoriented, overwhelmed. The first-days entries come from here. She learns the basics of the language, meets Manami, sees her first hatching, eats her first meal. Returns to England changed.

- **1948 --- Trip 2. Pepper alone. Age 47. ~6 months.**
  Ten-year gap. The war, then post-war chaos, rationing, the difficulty of disappearing across the Pacific when the world is watching. Cendre is 18, River is 15.
  The return. She's older, steadier, knows what to expect. Her Manaïari is functional now. She can have conversations. This is where the research deepens --- blue stone properties, the Tapestry of Time, the Knotted Cord (she observes but doesn't yet fully understand). Shorter trip because the children are approaching adulthood and Cassius needs her.

- **1955 --- Trip 3. Pepper alone. Age 54. ~1 year.**
  Seven-year gap. Cassius died in 1954 --- a quiet illness, painless. Cendre is 25, River is 22 --- both adults, both beginning their own careers.
  The immersion. This is the big trip. A full year. She receives her amulet. She witnesses the Humming Season. She joins the dental brushing line. She tries dream-fishing. She writes the richest, most detailed entries. Most of the journal's middle section comes from this trip. She's experienced enough to see deeply, young enough to climb mountains and sit on cliff edges.

- **1963 --- Trip 4. Pepper with Cendre (33) and River (30). ~6 months.**
  Eight-year gap. Pepper is 62. She brings both her children for the first time.
  Cendre approaches Aizomea the way his father would --- methodically, scientifically. He measures things. He takes samples. He asks precise questions. The Manaïari find him polite but puzzling.
  River approaches it differently. She walks into the village and exhales, as if she's been holding her breath for thirty years. She falls in love with the place instantly, viscerally. And she meets a Manaïari man. We don't know the details --- Pepper's journal is discreet on this point. But something sparks. When they leave after six months, River is quieter than when she arrived.

- **1971 --- Trip 5. Pepper (70) and River (38). ~1 year.**
  Eight-year gap. Pepper's last trip. She knows it --- her body is slower, the voyage is harder, and there's a finality in how she writes about this visit. She sees things she's seen before but describes them differently, with the tenderness of someone saying goodbye.
  River returns to Aizomea specifically. She reconnects with the Manaïari man she met eight years earlier. The reunion is not described in the journal --- Pepper gives her daughter privacy --- but there are gaps in the diary where days pass unrecorded, and when the entries resume, Pepper's tone is warmer, lighter, as if something good has happened nearby.
  Jamie is conceived during this trip. River returns to England pregnant. Pepper returns knowing she will not go back.

### After the Trips

- **1972** Jamie's birth. River's child, fathered by a Manaïari man.
  Same year: Dragon Protection Act, which Pepper has spent decades lobbying for. "Coincidentally, the year of my birth," Jamie writes later. The coincidence is probably not a coincidence --- Pepper, knowing her grandchild would be half-Manaïari, may have pushed harder than ever.

- **1985** Lady Pepper's death at 84.
  Full set of teeth (the blue stone paste). Unsent letters to Cassius in a drawer. A compass that still points somewhere that isn't north.

- **~2000** River's death at 67. Plane crash during an exploration. She followed in her mother's footsteps, became an explorer, but never returned to Aizomea. (Why not? The journal doesn't say. Perhaps the Manaïari man asked her not to bring the outside world closer. Perhaps she was afraid to go back. Perhaps she was waiting for Jamie to be old enough to go together.)

- **~2001** Cendre's death at 71. A few months after River. He died in his sleep. There was nothing wrong with him, the doctors said. His desk was tidy, his affairs in order. Jamie doesn't elaborate.

- **2004** Jamie (32) inherits the estate in Brittany. Finds the map, the trunk, the compass, Lady Chestnut. The journals. The research. Her grandmother's life's work, buried under the oldest tree.

- **20??** Jamie goes to Aizomea. Perhaps to see the world her grandmother loved. Perhaps to meet her father. Perhaps both.

- **2026** The book.

## Notes

- **Cassius:** Dies in 1954, aged ~54. A quiet illness, something painless --- he simply faded. Cendre is 24, River is 21. Old enough to manage. This explains why Trip 3 (1955) is so total --- a full year, the deepest immersion. Pepper has less pulling her home. The journal may not mention his death directly, but the tone of Trip 3 might carry it --- a woman writing with the particular freedom of someone who has nothing left to rush back to. Not bitter. Just untethered.

- **Jamie's father:** Never named in the timeline or the prologue. Jamie may not know his name until she reaches Aizomea. Or she may have always known --- River could have told her --- but never had a way to reach him. Either way, his existence is the emotional engine of Jamie's eventual journey.

- **The journal spans five trips across 33 years (1938-1971).** Pepper ages from 37 to 70 within its pages. Her voice should evolve --- younger Pepper is more excitable, more catalogue-minded, more English. Older Pepper is softer, more poetic, more willing to sit with mystery. The entries we've written so far blend these voices. As we place them in the timeline, we should consider which trip each comes from.

- **The book Jamie publishes is a curated selection.** Not every journal entry, not every trip. Jamie has chosen what to include --- which means she's also chosen what to leave out. The gaps are deliberate. Her editorial hand is mostly invisible, except for the occasional footnote (like the dental hygiene editor's note).
