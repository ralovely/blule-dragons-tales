# Early tone research — creative references

Five older-model documents supplied by the author on 29 October 2026, preserved unchanged with their downloaded filenames. The author generated them from other sources they provided. Treat them as supplied creative references: their value here is the inspiration and craft possibilities they convey, not scholarly attribution or citation traceability. Unlike the [Wyrmspan facts](../wyrmspan-dragon-facts.md), they do not impose world-fact compatibility constraints.

**Author direction: take a LOT of inspiration from Dautremer's books.** “Dautremer” here means the books as a whole: illustrations, text, atmosphere, humour, page composition and their interplay. Individual text authorship is not a relevant distinction for this creative purpose. This is a major influence, not merely a historical curiosity or an occasional technique to use sparingly.

Read the [assessment](../../workbench/notes/early-tone-research-assessment.md) for practical strengths and adaptations to this book. Its editorial judgments are recommendations, not a veto over the author's chosen influences. The approved manuscript and documented author decisions remain the baseline; applying the references does not by itself authorise unrequested rewrites or new canon.

The expandable [Notes for design](../../workbench/notes/notes-for-design.md) collects possibilities for text, illustration and page composition, beginning with the Dautremer-inspired discussion. These remain exploratory until the author chooses a direction.
