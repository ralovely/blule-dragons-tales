# Wyrmspan Dragon Facts — early-canon compatibility

## Source and authority

[Original Dragon Facts CSV](32fafdfa-wyrmspan_dragon_facts.csv), supplied by the author from the Wyrmspan game on 29 October 2026. The CSV is preserved unchanged; its Wyrmspan IDs provide stable row references.

Author direction: this is early canon to stay close enough to, not a requirement to reproduce every card fact. Use it with the [living canon ledger](canon-ledger.md) and [chronology](chronology.md). If a wider-world constraint appears to conflict with an approved story decision, flag the specific conflict for the author rather than silently changing either source or manuscript.

## How to use it

- **Wider-world statements are compatibility constraints.** Before making a relevant worldbuilding decision, read the source rows and ensure the proposal does not contradict their implications for history, institutions, society, or the place of dragons in the world. Compatibility does not require inserting those details into the book.
- **Dragon names are optional.** Lady Pepper would not have known the game's names. Retain the manuscript's naming freedom; do not infer that she knew a later classification or common name.
- **Most species anecdotes are optional.** Individual habits, jokes, encounters, and species-specific traits need not be imported. A trait of one species is not a rule for all dragons. Check anecdotes for wider-world implications before setting them aside.
- **Separate world facts from narrator knowledge.** Later discoveries and modern references describe the source's wider world, not necessarily what Pepper knew during her journeys. Preserve dated discoveries as such; allow period-appropriate observation without giving her future knowledge.
- **Preserve uncertainty.** A legend, belief, disputed theory, or reported encounter remains that kind of claim, not an established mechanism or universal truth.
