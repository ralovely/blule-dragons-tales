## Author-selected emotional additions

The author clarified the task's focus: Jamie's voice, not the prologue's alternation between biography and memory. On rereading, the assessment is that the sustained voices are sufficiently distinct: Indigo questions, tests and argues on the page; Jamie tells the reader a family story, with retrospective judgement and self-puncturing humour. Their shared wit need not be removed. The earlier recommendation to repair the biographical transitions was withdrawn. More emotion can come from Jamie's response to existing memories, without a new family event.
