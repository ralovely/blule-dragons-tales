## 3. World mechanics (must survive verbatim in meaning)

- **The compass**: sphere the size of a tangerine, carved dragon scales, blue-tinged metal band etched with stars, opens like a locket; inside a dragon's eye, green-gold, slit pupil; you look *through* it and one slice of horizon is brighter/more vivid — turn until the colours sing (P, 19). It does not point north; "It was looking" — likely the blue stone's call (31).
- **The blue stone**: hums (eggs hum **in pairs**); source = caves inside the floating islands; harvest is the **only accepted reason a human ever rides a dragon** — all-women riders who sing on the ascent (09). Harvesters' fingertips/palms stain permanently blue (05, 09). Mountains are blue at the peaks — stained memory of stone that has risen (09). Uses: pendants/amulets, thimbles, cooking pots, tools, mortar of glowing walls, poultice (12, 29), toothpaste (28), pigment (12), ice cream (62), gourd-voices (06). Glows at night, brighter at full moon (69); nesting dragons' teeth glow as a perimeter fence (28).
- **The amulet**: blue-stone hook worn by every Manaïari; a promise made touching it cannot be broken; conferred when ready, not at an age; losing one unthinkable (24); forged white-hot by fire dragon then cracked-cold by ice dragon — most stones shatter (25); all glow during the humming season (26). Indigo's still glows annually in the trunk (26 note).
- **The humming season**: dragons curl and hum; a single chord re-tunes the world and keeps the floating islands afloat; offerings of spiced honeyed milk; days of stillness (26). Triggers the fluffy species' allergy = Runny Nose Season (27).
- **Island concealment**: Fog-Weavers ring the coast (thin in the south, deliberately — 20); sentinel watchers above; fog puts crews to sleep; Ship-Shifters carry vessels past, set down on course; small kindnesses (fish, tailwind, rain in the tanks); the Elder blinks/nods; the nodded-at are carried to a village (18, 19).
- **Arrival rule of thumb**: "You do not get to audition" (18). Some Manaïari leave the island ("quiet returns", 14; the young man at the water's edge, 32; "a few leave; most come back").
- **The south**: purist dragons; the Tangle (wasps, sound-grasping vines, hiccup meadow); not hidden — *left alone*; a handful of humans reached it over the centuries; "None of them left. The tales do not say what happened to them. Nobody asks." (20). **Never resolve the mystery.**
- **The gift economy**: no money, no ownership-word, gold worthless ("It's soft. It bends. What would you do with it?"), favours remembered not tracked (04, 10). *Tension with merchant vocabulary in 17 & 61 — fix 10.*
- **Listeners**: warm palms, mostly women, ~30 in the village; knowledge arrives as weather, "like hearing colour"; 14 words for dragon silence, 7 learnable (05). Listeners translate dragon stories at the Knotted Cord (21).
- **Sound instruments**: seashell (carries over water), clicked stones (through rock), feather-string hum (shy species), gourds incl. blue-dust voice; response is movement, not sound; "It's not the note. It's the breath behind it." (06).
- **Language**: water-over-stones sound; formal register near dragons; emotions/weather are visitors; no "own/possess"; the tense for things whose effects persist; dragon-sound loanwords; no written form (Indigo first to transcribe); place names revise themselves; paths warm with use; blue ≈ home (04).
- **Dragons**: do not speak, ever (05). Body language: bared teeth = hello; tail grammar; circling = something important (incl. naps); one leg = dissent-without-argument (58). Boredom stages: tail, rearrangement, stare, grin; cure is a job (59). Memory: epochs yes, breakfast no — hence the Knotted Cord exchange: mayflies for mountains (21). Hoards = knick-knack autobiographies, never gold (42). Deaths: fade / swim out / fly to a floating island / slow petrification (the hills have faces); some "become the island" (43). Nest-gardens tended after death (44). Mouelleubleus: blue depth = age; dust-cheats fool no one (45). Dream-fishing (46). Sky-calligraphy by females; Indigo read it upside down (50). Lantern Keepers (51). The collective sigh, unexplained (30). Mimics (60). Eggs: hundreds of kinds; floaters on strings; most never hatch → stone, furniture; doorstops sometimes hatch years later (33, 34). Hatchlings drink goat milk and dragon-milk sap (35). Kintsugi shells = birth records (36).
- **The riding rule** (09) vs **being carried** (18): distinct acts — riding is harvest-only; carrying is the dragons' own act (Ship-Shifters, the rite of arrival). **Fix 14: no change, author decision.**
- **Asleep-yet-remembers-the-nod** (18): deliberate paradox, island magic. **Fix 11: no change; preserve paradox.**
