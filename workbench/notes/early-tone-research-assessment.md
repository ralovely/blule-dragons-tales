## Overall judgment

Useful creative material, with Dautremer's books at the centre of the desired inspiration. Their age alone does not disqualify them. The useful assessment question is what each opens up for this book, and how to translate its effects without mechanically following every prescription. Some advice is broad or absolute; that calls for editorial judgment, not dismissal of the references.

The most useful shared idea is **a world encountered through a particular person's practical attention**, with ordinary routines continuing independently of that person. Alongside it, Dautremer's books offer a rich creative direction: archival playfulness, visual abundance, deadpan absurdity, intimate detail and melancholy. Existing examples in the manuscript give us a foothold for developing these qualities, not a ceiling on how much inspiration to take.

Comparison basis: current entries 02, 04, 12, 23, 31, 74 and 77; the [canon ledger](../../reference/canon-ledger.md); and the authority, settled-work and editorial-constraint sections of the [master plan](../plans/editorial-revision-plan.md). ## Value by document

### Worldbuilding through character — highest practical value

**Keep:** what someone notices, takes for granted, misunderstands or needs tells us about both person and place. Routine and cultural friction can convey a world without an explanatory lecture. This is a useful diagnostic lens for Pepper's field journals.

**Adapt:** the “tourist trap” warning concerns implausible explanation by insiders. Pepper actually is an outsider and a scientist recording discoveries. Her sustained attention is legitimate; it should change with familiarity rather than disappear. Entry 02's child stepping over a dragon gives her a practical way to learn; entry 12 shows how much has become ordinary by T3.

**Adapt:** multiple viewpoints are an optional technique, not a reason to add narrators. “Show, don't tell” is too absolute for journals: explanation, summary and direct reflection belong here too. Use the literary examples to explore possibilities, rather than as exclusive accounts of how those books work.

### Matthew Mercer guide — useful scene toolkit, best used flexibly

**Keep:** “What was happening here five minutes before the characters arrived?” is a productive scene question. Repairs, ongoing work, personal preoccupations and dialogue that reveals more than its speaker intends can make a setting feel inhabited. Contrast can convey scale without superlatives. Entry 02 already explicitly presents a village whose life does not adjust to Pepper's arrival; entry 04 lets Manami's teasing carry cultural information and relationship together.

**Adapt:** choose sensory details through Pepper's attention, not a checklist. Clothing, posture and behaviour can reveal character, but not every arrival needs a full physical inventory. The very short entries 23 and 74 demonstrate how little description a complete effect may need.

**Use as exercises, not compulsory formulas:** the five-stage sensory arrival and six-stage character introduction can help generate material. Vary or discard their order in the finished prose. “Vision is … least interesting” is a useful provocation to notice other senses, not a reason to suppress the visual attention of a scientist and illustrator. The value of the examples here lies in the effects they suggest.

**Low relevance here:** combat translation, escalating body horror and routine cliffhangers. Those techniques belong to particular effects, not a default travel-journal voice. They do not justify revealing the south's protected off-page danger or turning quiet entry endings into suspense hooks.

### Dautremer guide — major creative influence

**Author direction:** take a LOT of inspiration from these books. This encompasses text, illustrations, atmosphere, page composition and their interplay. “Dautremer's books” is the author's creative reference category; separating the contributions by authorship does not help this task.

**High-value possibilities:** calmly delivered absurdity; intimate, peculiar domestic detail; humour sharing space with melancholy; mock-scholarship that treats improbable subjects seriously; lists, annotations and cross-references that make a book feel discovered and inhabited. The scrapbook sensibility is especially fertile for Pepper's journals. Text and image can complement, complicate or quietly contradict one another instead of merely repeating the same information. These are directions to explore substantially, not decorative touches to ration.

**Translate to this book:** use wordplay where it belongs to Pepper's ear and bilingual perspective. Let the juxtaposition of the fantastic and the practical do the work that overt anachronism does in a fairy tale. Explore marginal and visual forms when layout work is in scope, without assuming every entry needs a sidebar. Keep Pepper and her relationships emotionally complex: the guide's preference for concept-shaped characters can inspire a vignette or comic portrait without flattening the central people. Entry 77's family conflict is a strength to carry into this influence, not an obstacle to it.

**Overall:** one of the richest references for the book's creative identity. The adaptation question is how its abundance, humour, intimacy and sadness can live through Pepper's journal, not whether the influence has been academically demonstrated.

### Writing style sources — useful influence map

**Keep:** it names recognisable areas of interest: Chabat's incongruity, Carroll's verbal logic, Shadok persistence, Ghibli's everyday activity and pauses, and the emotional/visual atmosphere of illustrated books.

**Adapt:** the broad categories help name desired effects, but need not become rigid definitions. Kishōtenketsu can prompt structural exploration without requiring the existing book to adopt a new four-part scheme. Likewise, stillness is a pacing possibility, not a requirement to make every piece quiet.

**Use:** a vocabulary for discussing influences and choosing combinations. The author supplied these summaries from their own source material; rebuilding their bibliography is not a prerequisite for using them creatively.

### Writing style / “The Alchemic Pen” — useful synthesis, needs choices in application

**Keep:** the desired coexistence of grounded wonder, play, affection, melancholy and implication. Its reminder that characters can treat fantastic events matter-of-factly fits Aizomea well.

**Adapt:** it offers a wide range of registers and devices, from minimalism to lyrical prose, satire, pop culture, dream logic, puzzles and emotional depth. Its practical limitation is that it leaves the choice between them to the writer. Read it as a palette rather than a recipe requiring every ingredient.

**Risk:** literal application would produce a generic “whimsical” blend rather than Pepper's particular voice. Modern pop-culture gags, compulsory wordplay, arbitrary rule-breaking and explaining the world's mechanisms are not automatically appropriate. The accepted text sometimes depends on a deliberate unresolved paradox or silence. Entry 31 ends with a personal address, not a lesson or mechanism; entry 74 leaves the shoulders touching to do their work.

## What future agents should take from this

As editorial recommendations, subject to the author's decisions:

1. Treat the supplied files as creative references and Dautremer's books as a major intended influence. Explore their possibilities actively; attribution and citation traceability are not gates to their use.
2. Prefer a specific observed act, practical consequence or object when it can carry world and relationship together. Keep direct explanation where the journal needs it.
3. Let humour and feeling coexist without requiring both in every entry. Preserve the manuscript's range, warmth, exuberance and deliberate unevenness; restraint is not a universal target.
4. Preserve the distinction between Pepper's interpretation and world fact. Neither absurdity nor a research guide licenses new canon.
5. Retain the original documents unchanged. A future house-style guide can combine these influences with author-selected passages and decisions, translating their effects into this book's own voice and form.

The author's clarification establishes the creative direction above. This assessment does not itself implement manuscript or layout changes.
