## Reading key

- **L**: established in the living ledger as well as manuscript; **M**: explicit existing manuscript claim, even when the ledger's inventory is incomplete. M is not an unapproved proposal.
- **O**: narrated observation/experience; **R**: explicitly reported information or belief; **I**: inference, estimate or personal interpretation. Unqualified explanatory statements are marked **assertion**, not retrospectively recast as measured results. These labels describe presentation, not a hierarchy authorising deletion.
- Each row is a distinct occurrence or claim anchor; multiple claims in one sentence stay together where separating them would obscure the qualification. Quoted anchors are exact baseline wording. **Keep / none** means no wording change and no canon amendment requested.
- Resonance/response, prepared craft, long contact/incorporation and dragon-mediated behaviour are useful filing families only. They are not manuscript terminology, exhaustive causes, stages every object must undergo, or new laws.

## Initial coherence judgment — superseded after author clarification

The existing vocabulary already carries the connections: faint vibration/hum/call; dust/ground/crushed/paste; glint/glow; pigment that enters some surfaces over time; specific human/dragon work. The resource is widely used, not rare, but access, fragility, labour and the amulet's high failure rate make it hard-won. No price, scarcity regime or devotional institution needs adding.

**Propose only A, 07: “a familiar reverence” → “a familiar respect”.** This changes the register of the quarry reference to match the approved framing without diminishing wonder, the women's harvest rite, the still hands or the offerings. It is a wording proposal for author review, not a factual amendment. Leave every other occurrence unchanged.

No canon amendment is requested. Apparent tensions do not warrant silently changing facts:

- Heavy raw specimens versus one floating shell: both existing claims stand. Inventing a state-change mechanism, weakening the causal egg sentence or extending buoyancy to gliders would require an author decision. None is needed for this pass.
- Fragile harvest versus durable tools: a specialised amulet transformation is already shown; there is no requirement to specify the making of every pot, thimble and tool. Do not universalise that transformation.
- Lower caves versus sky source: 09 identifies the true source/accepted harvest, not the sole place any trace can exist. 42 remains an observed cave with blue stone. A universal geological history or restriction would be a new claim.
- Permanent hands, a day's blue smile and transferable cosmetic dust describe different surfaces and contexts. No invented processing/staining law is required to preserve all three.
- Daily faint light, stronger cave light, full-moon response and annual amulet signal remain distinct existing descriptions. Neither a lunar trigger for the humming season nor a universal light ceiling follows.
- 32 explicitly distinguishes local belief from Pepper's compass inference. 44 distinguishes local belief from grief. Leave those source boundaries intact.

The author need only accept, revise or reject A. If they want a wider reconciliation instead, its proposed new facts must return as explicit decisions outside the manuscript/canon, not be smuggled in as wording.
