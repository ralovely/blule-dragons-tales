*25th May 1938*

*(The spiders spin between the horns overnight. By morning, most dragons are wearing lace. Some shake it off. Margaret here has kept hers since Tuesday.)*

*(These beetles pulse. In rhythm, like a heartbeat. The shells are infused with blue stone. At dusk, the grass is full of them. Like walking through a fallen sky.)*

*(Nests in the fur, just behind the jaw. Neither party has acknowledged the other in the three weeks I have been watching. A perfect arrangement.)*
