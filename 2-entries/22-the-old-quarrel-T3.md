*12th September 1955*

There is an old dragon in the square with four long scars down his left flank, straight and parallel and gone pale with age. Hunters did not make them; I know that catalogue by heart. Those were claws, and no small ones.

I asked Manami, once, at the racks. "He went south once," she said, and in passing let two fingers rest on one of the cords: an ugly knot, two strands forced through each other, singed at one end. She did not take it down.
