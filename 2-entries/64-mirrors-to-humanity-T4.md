Dragons watch us. I don't mean with suspicion or hunger. I mean with interest. It is the interest a child takes in some pointless adult task, deciding it must be worth doing after all.

Yesterday, a man fell asleep in his hammock after lunch. Nothing unusual. But a dragon had been watching from the ridge, and this morning, the same dragon had wedged itself between two palm trees in a cargo net stolen from the docks. It was swaying dangerously. It looked deeply uncomfortable and absolutely committed to the idea. By the end of the week, three more dragons had done the same. The Manaïari have started reinforcing their trees. River watched the whole thing unfold with plain delight, like someone who had always suspected the world worked this way and was pleased to see it proven. Manami was watching River. She said nothing, but she had the look of someone recognising a younger version of somebody she knows.
