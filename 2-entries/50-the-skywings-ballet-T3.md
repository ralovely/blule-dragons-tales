*29th March 1956*

I watched a young dragon try to join a formation today. It was half a beat behind on every turn, banking too late, climbing too steeply. The others continued, opening around it as a river flows around a stone. By the third pass, the young one had found the rhythm. By the fifth, I could no longer tell which one it was. They taught by making space at exactly the right moment.

Some species perform at night. I sat on a ridge after dark and watched them cross the sky, their scales catching the moonlight and leaving trails behind them. Silver arcs, fading slowly. I sat there long after they had gone, watching the last trails dissolve.
