It happens without warning. On still, humid afternoons, every dragon in the valley exhales at the same moment. A long, low sigh that lasts perhaps three seconds. There is no wind, no movement. But for a moment I could have sworn my hair shifted, and the air tasted different. The dragons don't look at each other afterwards. They don't seem to have coordinated. They all felt the same thing at the same time. Not a soul can explain it. I asked a shaman. I asked the elders. I asked everyone I could find. They all shrugged.

For about ten minutes after, everything is slightly sharper. Colours a touch brighter. The cliff moss smells stronger. My tea tasted better, though it was the same cup I had been drinking before. Then it fades, and the world returns to normal, and you are left wondering whether the dragons just cleaned something you cannot see, or reset it.

Cassius, you would have measured it. I just sat there.

*(Editor's note: My grandfather Cassius died in 1954. She never stopped writing to him.)*
