*7th September 1938*

There was a festival last night. Hundreds of people, dozens of dragons, music I am only beginning to understand. I sat in the middle of it and realised I am the only person here who has a world outside this one. Everyone else is home. I am visiting. The difference is enormous and invisible. Manami sat down beside me at some point. "You are thinking about them," she said. It was not a question.
