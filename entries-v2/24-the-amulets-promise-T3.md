Every Manaïari I have met wears the same hook of blue stone around their neck. It is an anchor and a compass, not an ornament. A promise made while touching the amulet, I am told, cannot be broken. It is a piece of the island's very heart, worn against their own.

They are not given out on a birthday, or at a specific age. A child receives their amulet when the elders (and, it seems, the dragons themselves) deem them *ready*. It is a rite of passage measured not in years, but in maturity and understanding. To receive one is to be fully seen, to belong not just to a family but to the land itself. To lose one, I am told, is unthinkable. No one would explain what happens. They just touched their own and changed the subject. I wonder if Nangula had one. I never saw her wear it, but then she was good at keeping things close.
