Nangula. Your compass is out on the desk tonight. The eye is as bright as the day it found me; we are leaving soon, and it knows the way home better than I do.

You waited half a lifetime to be sure of me. I understand that waiting better now. I have begun to look at how people hold things. River holds them as if they had been lent to her.

I polished the eye, which it does not need, and put it away.
