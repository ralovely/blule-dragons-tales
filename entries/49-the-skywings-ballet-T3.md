I watched a young dragon try to join a formation today. It was half a beat behind on every turn, banking too late, climbing too steeply. The others did not stop. They did not slow down. They adjusted around it, the way a river flows around a stone, and kept going. By the third pass, the young one had found the rhythm. By the fifth, I could no longer tell which one it was. I have not seen anything taught so gently. No correction. No instruction. Just space, made at exactly the right moment.

Some species perform at night. I sat on a ridge after dark and watched them cross the sky, their scales catching the moonlight and leaving trails behind them. Silver arcs, fading slowly, like letters in a language I almost recognised. I sat there long after they had gone, watching the last trails dissolve.
