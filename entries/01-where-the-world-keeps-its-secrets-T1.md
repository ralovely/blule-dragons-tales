Here I am, scribbling away in my journal. The scent is a peculiar mix of damp earth and something distinctly... draconic. Not fiery, mind you, but warm and ancient, like sunlight on old stone. If you were to ask me exactly where I am, Cassius, I'd have to wave my hands vaguely towards the great, shimmering expanse of the Pacific Ocean.

Imagine drawing a curled-up serpent on a map, somewhere between New Zealand and South America. Then, imagine it as a brand new landmass with secrets tucked into every fold. That's Aizomea for you, a place that delights in being just beyond definition, yet utterly, undeniably, here.

Its edges are never quite the same from one day to the next, dissolving into a pearl-grey mist that smells of salt and eucalyptus haze, only to reform into a jagged coastline of deep blue cliffs. From above, the whole thing resembles a contented, slumbering dragon --- its spine a range of cobalt mountains, its belly a patchwork of plains and sparkling rivers. I've tried to sketch it twice now. Both times it had moved by the time I put my pencil down. A continent with no interest in being mapped.

And yet, when I feel the soft moss beneath my feet and hear the distant, happy rumble of a sleeping dragon, I know it's the most real place I've ever been.

*Day twelve.*
